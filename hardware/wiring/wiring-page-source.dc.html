CHECK FIRST, WITH THE MULTIMETER
1. Motor, at its plug (Ω): black–green and red–blue 2–3 Ω each; black–red reads nothing.
2. Plug and harness in the nuts, plug out (Ω): + nut to − nut must not read 0.
3. Plug in, key to ACC (V DC, red probe on +): about 12 V, no minus sign. Key off, plug out.
4. Board in, no power (Ω): + to − still not 0; pink to blue about 120 Ω (the board's own).
FIRST POWER-UP
5. Motor gear out of the teeth. Plug in, key on: the board may turn the motor slowly to set itself up. If its blue light stays on, press F1. Then plug in the comma four.
GOOD TO KNOW
Pink and blue join the wires to the board with the inline lever nuts, one wire in each end. No extra resistor: left out on purpose. The only fuse is the 15 A one in the plug (the socket takes 10 A; this draws under 4 A). Extend pink and blue with one twisted pair (old ethernet cable).
